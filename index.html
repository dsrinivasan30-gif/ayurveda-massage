Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our therapies and male client policy

Are services available for female clients or couples?

No, bookings are Exclusively For Male Clients. SVN Re+Life operates as a dedicated sanctuary exclusively for men's wellness, pain management, and stress relief. All therapies are personally conducted by certified male therapist Yuvaraj R.

What kind of oils are used?

We only use authentic, unadulterated Ayurvedic medicated herbal oils (such as Mahanarayana, Dhanwantharam, Ksheerabala, and Murivenna) formulated with medicinal herbs to treat specific bodily conditions.

Will oil stain my home furniture or floor?

No. Our therapist sets up multi-layer waterproof and disposable hygienic protective sheets beneath the massage setup, ensuring your home remains completely clean.

How should I prepare before the session?

It is best to have had a light meal at least 1 hour before therapy. Keep a warm towel ready if you wish, and ensure the room is warm, well-ventilated, and quiet.

What should I do after the massage?

Allow the healing herbal oils to absorb into your body for 30–45 minutes. Because every body constitution (dosha) and therapy differs, our therapist will personally evaluate you and advise whether warm or room-temperature water is right for your bath. Avoid harsh chemical soaps and heavy physical work for optimal rejuvenation.

How many sessions are recommended for back pain?

While many clients feel noticeable relief after a single session, a course of 3 to 7 sessions of Kati Basti with Abhyanga is recommended for chronic stiffness or sciatica.

How do I book an appointment?

You can click the "Book an Appointment" button on this site to view all massage therapies and pricing, then call to book or send a query directly via WhatsApp enquiry (+91 99404 67055) for instant support.
